What repair-OneDrive.ps1 reports per user and per machine: OneDrive's status, problems it detected and the restarts it did.

Paste a SAS URL of the onedrivesyncstatus table with Read permission (sp=r). With Read and Delete (sp=rd) you can also delete old reports here.
  • The storage account needs a CORS rule on the Table service: allowed origin *, methods GET, HEAD, OPTIONS (and POST, DELETE to delete old reports), allowed and exposed headers *.
  • The SAS URL stays in this browser tab and is only sent to the storage account.
  • Users and machines that didn't report in the chosen period are left out. Redeployed session hosts show up as a new machine under the same name.
